# Xiao-inspired internal review — ICRA methods reframe

**Scope:** full supplied `paper/icra/DRAFT.md` plus the local claim ledger, Phase-G table shell,
and draft preregistration. This is an independent rubric-guided audit, not Xuesu Xiao speaking,
an official ICRA review, or an acceptance prediction.

## Charitable reconstruction

The paper identifies reference--physics misalignment (RPM): an outcome-adaptive curriculum cannot
interpret persistent tracking error as useful difficulty when the final robot-space reference
demands unavailable contact wrench or actuator effort. CLIMB addresses the interface through a
three-stage method: screen final trajectories, route intervals by cause, then gate adaptive
allocation to exact non-wrapping support. Its strongest supported contribution is this complete
training interface, backed by failure anatomy, bank-scale measurements, cross-implementation
agreement, and a code-complete one-variable allocation test.

## Method-reframe audit

- **Changed component is visible:** `F_u` is a binary reference-physics gate; `LP_u` controls only
  allocation inside admitted support. This is more precise than branding the work as an audit.
- **Sampler equation matches code:** the cap operator jointly takes deployment prior, focus mass,
  and `rho`; it preserves the exact exploration floor while reallocating focus under unit/clip
  ceilings.
- **Zero-mechanism control is preserved:** Phase G changes G2 allocation relative to G1 on the
  same gate and legal-start table. Smooth weighting, repair, or dirty support must not be inserted
  into that experiment.
- **Novelty boundary is explicit:** physics-aware curation and dynamic retargeting already exist.
  The paper's delta is the final-trajectory admissibility gate coupled to exact-support adaptive
  allocation and a matched test of allocator value.
- **Claim escalation has a named experiment:** H1 changes only binary admission under a matched
  dirty bank. A title claiming prevention of curriculum collapse is reserved for a passed H1
  mechanism and policy-outcome test.

3. **§3 — rebuild detail.** The method now states the five-frame smoothing, 0.6 primary friction,

## Remaining prioritized concerns

1. **Validity-critical until resolved — allocator benefit.** No Phase-G endpoint exists. If
   calibration/validation fails, contribution 3 must remain a controlled test apparatus and the
   abstract must report `not_tested`. If it passes, every policy claim needs seed/clip units,
   intervals, manipulation checks, and provenance gates in the frozen result table.
2. **Major empirical gap — gate benefit under contamination.** Existing historical runs motivate
   RPM but do not isolate binary gating. H1 is required before claiming that CLIMB prevents
   collapse or preserves learning on a dirty corpus.
3. **Major scope boundary — repair.** DFRP proves that the route is executable on a stratified
   panel; N7 does not establish controller superiority. H3 must compare gate-only with
   gate-plus-certified-repair while measuring reference distortion and unchanged-motion
   regression.
4. **Reproducibility and presentation.** Figs. 1–2 are composed in the two-column source, the
   rebuild contract is stated, and the bibliography uses primary records. Re-run the page/font,
   arithmetic, anonymity, and artifact-route checks after Phase-G objects are populated.
