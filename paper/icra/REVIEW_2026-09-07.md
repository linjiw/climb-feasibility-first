# Evidence review of the completed-result manuscript

Unsealed review of `root.tex`, its seven-page PDF and the generated Markdown
companion. Applies the evidence-focused robotics writing rubric; does not
predict an individual reviewer's verdict.

The paper now asks which failures make useful practice and separates reference
admission from allocation. Its strongest supported contribution is measurement
and an exact temporal interface, with a complete controlled policy comparison.
The result is inconclusive; stronger allocation or admission claims are absent.

| Claim | Evidence and independent unit | Alternative explanation / remaining limit | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RPM can attract failed practice | Historical three-seed campaign, A5/A7/N1; local unsupported descent | Non-floor bug and reference defects are part of this historical setting; not a general causal estimate | Scoped to diagnosed campaign |
| Final-trajectory screen is useful measurement | 2,442/10,705 and 7/4,950; 39/40 same-input decisions | Different banks/pipelines are not causal retargeter comparisons; enriched panel is not prevalence | Separate denominators and qualification retained |
| Feasibility features transfer difficulty | 100 held-out clips; 0.567→0.609, p=.010 against 200 random feature triples | Same learner architecture; not hardware or cross-architecture transfer | Scope explicit |
| R changes exposure | Twelve training gates, 492 saved states, deterministic replay | Exposure is not control utility | Apparatus claim only |
| R improves policy | Three independent paired seeds, 48 cells, 134,400 conditions | Wide seed interval; bootstrap cannot create independent policies | Inconclusive, no benefit/equivalence/harm inference |
| R accelerates learning | Four sparse checkpoints; exploratory AULC negative in 3/3 pairs | Target attainment is exploratory, two non-attainments; no precise crossover | Efficiency claim removed |
| Repair improves control | One frozen policy, 26 clips, 656 paired conditions per arm | Changes the target; one-policy clip uncertainty, no retraining test | Qualification separated from inconclusive exploratory deployment |
| Admission helps learning | Separate H1 prospectively frozen, not yet measured | Shared support U/A/R/D cannot answer it | Not a current contribution |

The abstract, conclusion, main paired table and learning-curve caption agree on
the result. A failed broad non-regression guard is not reported as proof of harm.
The observed t half-width is a precision calculation, not a power guarantee.
The screen is model-relative and not a physical feasibility certificate. The
current simulator's torque, delay and terrain assumptions are disclosed.

Related work credits motion curation, GMT/EGM's adaptive sampling, ALP-GMM and
Syllabus. The last two were checked against their primary paper/official
implementation documentation on September 7; normalization alone is not a
novelty claim. Sources:
[ALP-GMM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07224),
[Syllabus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11318),
[Syllabus curriculum documentation](https://ryannavillus.github.io/Syllabus/curricula/implemented_curricula.html).

Remaining review-sensitive limitations: the strongest behavioral result is
historical, the controlled allocator result has three pairs, H1 is pending,
continuous/hardware transfer is untested, and anonymous artifact access plus
final author approval are not complete. These limitations should determine the
submission claim; none is repaired by adding another allocation plot.
