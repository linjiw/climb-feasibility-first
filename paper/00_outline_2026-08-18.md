# CLIMB — paper outline (single flagship), drafted 2026-08-18

Working title: **Where humanoid motion tracking fails, and why: feasibility × support × intrinsic
difficulty in curriculum learning over a validated 10,822-clip bank**

Spine (one argument, mechanism → intervention):
1. Failure-adaptive sampling collapses (87–89 % of exposure onto one clip in 3/3 seeds) and loses to
   uniform (held-out survival 0.780 vs 0.810; AULC 0.640 vs 0.698).
2. The "uniform floor" is not a floor: p = counts + ε/N gives the uniform share ε/(Σq+ε) < 1 % at
   realistic failure rates, num_envs-dependent; inherited upstream (mjlab #1153, whole_body_tracking #73).
3. The attractor is *unsupported and infeasible*: clip #44 sits in a category the bank barely contains
   (3.2 % of duration) and its retargeted descent is airborne (~329 N unsupported for a second, N1);
   family-wide (12/40 nearest neighbours). Neither physics-parameter fragility (G1: no ±δ changes
   survival) nor pure representation failure explains it.
4. Grounded repair (normalise-then-mix) restores coverage: entropy 0.61 vs 0.39, top-1 mass 0.57–0.70
   vs 0.87–0.89, AULC 0.696 ≈ uniform, endpoint 0.825 (3/3 seeds over uniform, secondary).
5. Composition as the causal fix (N3, pre-registered): 16 feasible kneel/crawl neighbours vs 16 random
   clips; keystone endpoint on stratified-start survival.
6. Support-moderation at scale (E3, sealed bidirectional predictions incl. named harms).

Method contributions carried alongside:
- The **feasibility screen** (contact-free ID + torque-limited contact LP): a physics-grounded
  retarget-quality feature; prevalence over 10,822 clips (in progress); upstream note.
- The **conformance protocol** (G0): per-substep paired stepping, MuJoCo-C as third referee, shadow
  solver stepping the other engine's state; four silent integration errors (DR mirror, float32
  geometry, stale obs, one-directional coupling) — the exposure taxonomy's fourth layer.
- The **calibrated fragility instrument** (N5): signed replicate-mean effects against a published
  identical-physics floor; the folded |Δφ| statistic is chaos-dominated (Lyapunov ~1–2 s at mm scale).
- The **stratified-start evaluation protocol**: start-offset-averaged survival confounds where a
  policy fails with how much of a clip is failable.

Decomposition figure (spine): difficulty = feasibility × support × intrinsic; each axis with its
measurement (contact LP · kNN support · atlas features) and its fix (repair/exclude · composition ·
curriculum/robustness). `figures/decomposition.svg`.
