# DFRP v0 — CPU-first implementation contract

**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Status:** unsealed implementation addendum. It is subordinate to sealed
Research Plan v5 and does not authorize new GPU training before 2026-09-15.

## Decision

Productize the existing screen, repair census, and exact segment runtime into a
single fail-closed artifact contract before revisiting curriculum benefit.
Scientific routing and training readiness are separate: historical artifacts
may establish that a clip is a repair candidate, but only a hash-matched exact
support sidecar and complete repair qualification can admit it to training.

## Frozen v0 policy

- Flat terrain, full-trajectory screen, contact gap 0.06 m.
- Repair entry: strict `infeasible_frac > 0.10`.
- Recovered: `infeasible_frac_after <= 0.05`.
- Primary repair tier: root displacement `<= 0.08 m`.
- Exploratory tier: `0.08 < displacement <= 0.15 m`; never primary training.
- Exact trial horizon: 50 steps; all advances stay inside a feasible segment.
- Routes: `raw_feasible`, `repair_primary`, `repair_exploratory`,
  `segment_only`, `quarantine`.
- Training requires exact support and one of the first, second, or fourth
  routes. Missing artifacts, mismatched hashes, incomplete legacy repair checks,
  and empty support fail closed.

`dfrp_bank_manifest/1` records input/model/tool hashes, screen and repair
metrics, continuous fidelity, route reasons, selected training motion, exact
sidecar, and a canonical payload hash. `tools/build_segment_unit_table.py`
accepts the manifest and embeds its payload identity and route into the existing
`segment_unit_table/1` sources.

## Sampler and evaluator contract

Keep the segment-native runtime: legal-start deployment mass, a fixed 0.10
simplex floor, conditional unit statistics, explicit 50-step truncation,
dedicated RNG/checkpoint state, and 0.05 unit/0.25 clip caps. Do not reintroduce
clip-level raw failure EMA. Primary evaluation remains paired and feasible-only;
four coarse phase offsets are compatibility diagnostics, not a substitute for
exact segment/start manifests.

## Gates

1. Synthetic contract fixtures cover no-op, primary/exploratory repair,
   residual repair, segment retention, quarantine, missing inputs, and tampering.
2. A stratified development panel compares raw, root-only, and root+IK outputs
   without using policy outcomes to select thresholds.
3. The 10,705-clip manifest reports the legacy census under the new 8/15 cm
   routing vocabulary while clearly marking incomplete legacy qualification.
4. No simulator smoke proceeds until selected motions and exact sidecars are
   hash-matched and `training_eligible`.
5. Every paper-bound number enters `paper/RESULTS_LOG.md`; sealed inputs remain
   untouched; all long jobs write completion sentinels.

## Deferred by this addendum

Newton v1.5 recertification follows DFRP v0. New training follows the existing
September 15 gate and requires a separate seal. Differentiable feasibility,
terrain SDFs, cross-embodiment work, hardware guards, and external PR submission
are not v0 work.
