## H4 → H4-r (revised): estimator fidelity, not band mass

**v2 H4:** "Curriculum benefit is moderated by frontier-band occupancy. Predicts:
benefit correlates with band mass/flux across runs."

**Retired because it has the wrong sign.** In Exp-1 the *losing* arm had the
*higher* band occupancy: adaptive 0.210 final band mass and 2.01 mean dwell,
against uniform's 0.150 and 1.47. Band mass conflates two opposite states —
clips teachable-and-in-transit, and clips **stuck** because the sampler is not
carrying them through. As a covariate it would have loaded on stuckness here.

**H4-r:** *Curriculum benefit is moderated by estimator fidelity* — the mean
per-clip discrepancy between sampler-implied failure and eval-measured failure.
Band flux is demoted from a model term to a reported health diagnostic.

Measurement note: full per-clip implied failure is **not** recoverable for the
Exp-1 arms. Checkpoints carry only `common_step_counter` (verified), and the logs
carry entropy plus top-1 identity and mass, not the whole distribution. H4-r is
therefore testable only from E10 onward, once the §7.1 ledger lands. Recorded
here so the gap is visible rather than discovered at write-up.

Supporting diagnostic, available now: **directional** band throughput. Total
crossings mislead — they count churn as progress. Split by direction, net
progress is near-identical across arms while downward crossings order cleanly
with coverage:

| arm | up / 1k | down / 1k | net |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| uniform | 26.1 | **5.6** | +20.5 |
| grounded | 29.1 | **7.7** | +21.4 |
| adaptive | 30.6 | **10.3** | +20.3 |

Adaptive relearns and re-loses clips at roughly twice uniform's rate. This is
the "catastrophic forgetting of mastered clips" fable.md §3.4 names, measured.

## E10 added: freshness vs liveness, plus a cap arm

Pre-registered separately in `PREREGISTRATION_E10.md` (sealed before any grounded
result was analysed). Slotted **immediately after E2**, sized for the pre-Sept-15
gap window that is too small for E3. E3 stays post-Sept-15.

Design is a 2×2 over {floor: none / ε-mixture} × {α: 0.01 / 0.10} — two cells
already exist from Exp-1 and are not re-run — **plus a per-clip cap arm**,
triggered by A7 under the advisor's stated conditional.

The conceptual refinement that reshaped it: "estimator responsiveness" decomposes
into *signal freshness* (α, per update) and *estimator liveness* (whether a clip
receives updates at all, set by its sampling mass). A global α cannot fix an
allocation problem — it adds variance to the hot clip while cold clips stay
near-frozen. The ε floor therefore does double duty: coverage for the learner,
refresh for the estimator.

## A7 result: attractor, not staleness — and the atlas misses it

- Dominant clip is **#44 in all six runs** (3 adaptive + 3 grounded seeds).
  Seed-invariant identity rules out a stochastic staleness artefact.
- Its measured survival under the **uniform control** is **0.3125** against a
  bank mean of 0.892 — dying after 1.14 s, tracking error 0.140 (~3× median). So
  it is neither unlearnable nor mastered: it is the *persistently hardest* clip,
  sitting inside the frontier band, and an unbounded count EMA has no mechanism
  to bound its share.
- **The atlas does not flag it**: required_mu 0.18 and peak GRF 1.42, both low.
  A concrete instance of the atlas's incompleteness, consistent with A3, and a
  usable example for the paper.

Consequence: the fix class is a per-clip **mass cap** (an atlas-free L1 variant),
now an E10 arm at `c = 5` (no clip above 5× uniform).
